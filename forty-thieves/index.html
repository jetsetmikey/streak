        How it works

        • You, today. Your past selves are the numbered dots.
        • Guards grab anyone who lingers in their yellow cone. Sneak up behind one and press Act to knock him out for 12 seconds.
        • The camera and the lasers raise the alarm. Police arrive 10 seconds later and the heist ends.
        • The lever in the security office cuts the power for 10 seconds.
        • A plate opens the door with its letter while someone stands on it. The vault needs both of its plates at the same moment.
        • Press Act to pick up loot. Carry it to the van. Gold is heavy.
