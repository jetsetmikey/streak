    The catch

    Close Call

    Every day, your character faces one deadly situation. You get one sentence to get them out of it.

    • Read closely. Every situation hides a clue, and the obvious move is often the one that kills you.
    • Be specific. The narrator takes you at your word. The narrator is Claude.
    • Survive and your character lives to face tomorrow's close call. Die, or miss a day, and they're gone for good.

    Graveyard

      How to play

      • One close call a day. Everyone gets the same one, and a new one arrives at midnight.
      • One sentence. Use what's in your pockets, your own body, and anything the situation mentions. Magic, superpowers and "it was all a dream" don't work.
      • Every situation hides a clue. After the verdict, the catch tells you what it was.
      • Luck runs out. A new character gets the benefit of the doubt. After 3 close calls the narrator judges fairly, after 10 strictly, and after 25 very strictly.
      • Miss a day and your character is gone. They faced it alone. A new character arrives the next day.
      • The narrator is Claude. Each verdict uses your own Claude account.
